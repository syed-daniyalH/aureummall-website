## Source precedence

When the documents disagree, the relevant category-specific document takes precedence over the condensed catalogue:

- The Residential Collection includes a 658 sq ft inner one-bedroom and a 1,381 sq ft inner two-bedroom that the master catalogue omits.
- The Commercial & Food Court brochure and factsheet publish first-floor shops at PKR 50,000 per sq ft; the master catalogue still shows PKR 45,000 per sq ft.
- The full commercial table includes the 15% confirmation stage even though one short food-court summary omits it.

The website therefore treats the category brochures as the current product registers and the factsheet as the cross-check for summary figures.

## Payment structures

- Apartments and offices: 10% booking, 15% confirmation, 55 monthly installments of 1%, and 20% on completion.
- Shops and food court: 10% booking, 15% confirmation, 42 monthly installments of 1%, 13% across six half-yearly installments, and 20% on completion.

## Claims and imagery rules

- All building and interior renders are artist's impressions.
- Floor plans follow current project drawings and remain subject to final approval.
- Amenities, hotel, technology features, access arrangements and specifications are planned and require developer confirmation.
- Do not promise fixed footfall, rental return or capital appreciation.
- The office floor drawing appears to label a 2,016 sq ft office, while the explicit office price table lists 1,009 sq ft. The website follows the price table and does not advertise 2,016 sq ft as available.
